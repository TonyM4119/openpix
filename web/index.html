Presets

Save the current look (effect, palette, colors, speed, brightness, per-string effects) and bring it back with one tap. Presets can also go in the playlist and holiday calendar.

Sleep timer

Holiday calendar

Picks the effect automatically by date. While a date range matches it overrides the effect and the playlist.

Segments

Run a different effect on part of a string, e.g. pixels 0-99 of output 1. Pixel numbers start at 0.

My colors

Playlist

Schedule

Turns the effects on and off by themselves. Live shows from xLights are not affected.

Days:

Sunset is worked out for the middle of your state. If it's a few minutes off for your town, adjust "Minutes after sunset".

Safety

The power limit dims everything evenly when the estimated draw goes over it. Your 5 V supply is rated 60 A; 50 A leaves headroom. Most 5 V pixels draw about 20 mA per color.

Test lights

Identify lights each string with as many white pixels as its output number (output 3 shows 3 white pixels), so you can tell which string is plugged in where. Tests switch off when the controller restarts.

Pixel type & DMX

DMX outputs are the 3-pin RS-485 terminals marked DMX OUTPUT. Each sends one full universe (512 channels) received over sACN/Art-Net. Clock-type pixels use the CLK terminal on each output.
