OpenPix

Status

Holiday effects

Plays on its own, no xLights needed. Saved, so it comes back after a power cut. Show data from xLights/FPP takes over automatically and the effect resumes 3 s after it stops.

My colors

Pick up to 4 colors, then choose 🎨 My colors above (or put it in the playlist).

Playlist

Rotate through effects automatically. While on, it replaces the single effect choice.

Schedule

Turns the effects on and off by themselves. Live shows from xLights are not affected.

Days:

Latitude/longitude are only used to work out sunset. Look up your town on a map (right-click → coordinates) — two decimals is plenty.

Safety

The power limit dims everything evenly when the estimated draw goes over it. Your 5 V supply is rated 60 A; 50 A leaves headroom. Most 5 V pixels draw about 20 mA per color.

Test lights

Identify lights each string with as many white pixels as its output number (output 3 shows 3 white pixels), so you can tell which string is plugged in where. Tests switch off when the controller restarts.

Outputs

UniverseStart chPixelsNullZig-zagBright %ReverseOrderLineEffect

Saves instantly. No reboot.

Network & general

Changing the IP moves the controller; this page will try to follow it.
